Questions, answered.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Every Elevate plan is month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime.

Do you take equity in my practice?

No. Elevate never takes equity. You keep full ownership and control of your practice; we work for you.

How is pricing set?

Plans are priced per office and billed monthly: Profit Builder is $1,999, Growth Engine is $2,499 and Practice Optimizer is $3,499. Business taxes and payroll processing are included.

What happens in the free practice assessment?

We review your accounting, team, operations and marketing, show you what's holding the practice back, and tell you where we'd start. There's no cost and no commitment.

Can you replace my bookkeeper, payroll provider and tax preparer?

Yes. One Elevate team handles dental bookkeeping, payroll and business tax filing, so you deal with one partner and one bill instead of three or more vendors.

Can you help me open a new practice?

Yes. Our de novo services cover entity setup, budgeting, hiring, systems, a website and marketing, so a new practice opens with the whole ATOM Framework in place.

What is the Elevate app?

It's your practice's own account at app.elevatepractices.com, set up and run by our team: one inbox for website chat, texts and social messages, review requests and replies, social post scheduling, and online booking forms.

Where are you based?

Elevate is headquartered in St. George, Utah, and works with dental practice owners across the U.S.
