Elevate Dashboards Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

This policy explains how Elevate Practices ("Elevate," "we") handles information through Elevate Dashboards.

Information we collect

When a client connects their QuickBooks Online company, we read financial report data: income and expense totals by account and by month. We also store the connection details QuickBooks provides so the connection can stay active. We do not collect patient information through QuickBooks, and we do not access bank or card credentials.

How we use it

We use this data only to prepare private financial dashboards and reports for that client, and to provide our consulting services to that client.

Where it's stored

Summarized figures are stored in Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) accounts controlled by Elevate, protected by Google's security and limited to authorized Elevate staff and the client's authorized users.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent client data, and we do not share it with third parties except service providers needed to run the service (such as Google and Intuit), or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep dashboard data while the client relationship is active. A client can ask us to disconnect QuickBooks and delete their data at any time by emailing chris@elevatepractices.com, and can also disconnect directly in QuickBooks Online under Settings > Apps.

Security

Access is limited to authorized people, connection credentials are stored in restricted script settings rather than in documents, and dashboards require a private link.

Changes

We will post any updates on this page with a new effective date.
