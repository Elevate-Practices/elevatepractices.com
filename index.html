Common questions.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Every Elevate agreement is month-to-month.

Do you take equity in the practice?

No. Elevate does not take equity. You keep full ownership and control of your practice.

How is pricing set?

Plans are priced per office and billed monthly: Profit Builder is $1,999, Growth Engine is $2,499 and Practice Optimizer is $3,499. Business tax returns and payroll processing are included.

What does the practice assessment cover?

We review your financials, staffing, systems and marketing, and show you where we would start. There is no cost or commitment.

Can Elevate replace our bookkeeper, payroll provider and tax preparer?

Yes. One Elevate team handles dental bookkeeping, payroll and business tax returns, so you work with one firm and one bill.

Do you work with new practices?

Yes. Our de novo services cover entity setup, budgeting, payroll, hiring, systems, a website and marketing.

What is the Elevate app?

Your practice's account at app.elevatepractices.com, configured and managed by our team. It holds the shared inbox for chat, texts and social messages, review requests and replies, social scheduling and online booking forms.

Where is Elevate based?

Elevate is headquartered in St. George, Utah, and works with dental practices across the United States.
